Interactive experiment 4
Watch Video Quality Change With Internet Speed
One student has fast Wi-Fi. Another is on a slow train connection. A video player can choose short segments at different qualities depending on the network. Change the internet speed below and fetch the next segment.
Quality: 720p
What does each term mean?
Segment: one short piece of a longer video, perhaps a few seconds.
Manifest: a small list telling the player which segments and qualities exist.
Transcoding: making a 1080p version, 720p version, 480p version and so on.
Adaptive bitrate: changing quality when the network changes.
The storage bucket holds these segments like ordinary files. The player decides which quality to request next.


Interactive experiment 5
Understand Where the Money Goes
Newbert stores a 1 GB workshop video. Storage is only one cost. If 10,000 students stream the entire video, the system delivers about 10,000 GB of data. Use the sliders to see why a download-heavy application needs a careful delivery plan.

A CDN cache hit can reduce origin traffic. It does not make the viewer's total downloaded bytes disappear. Actual bills depend on provider, region, request class and network agreements.
