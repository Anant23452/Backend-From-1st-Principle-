Easy explanation

Three ways to understand your backend

📖 Logs
What happened?

The diary of events. Example: a login was rejected at 10:30.

📊 Metrics
How much? How often?

Numbers plotted over time. Example: 50 failed requests in one minute.

🗺️ Traces
Where did it happen?

A map of one request’s work, including handlers, services, and database calls.

Remember: logs = diary, metrics = health dashboard, traces = journey map. The goal is to move from “something is wrong” to “here is the likely cause.”
